Widths from the real counts

Honest, and almost unreadable.

Regression holds 210 of the release's 277 records, so at true scale it is the only tier with any width and everything above it is a sliver. The violation that matters — System's 15 against Feature's 13 — is a 2-pixel difference nobody can see.

It does say one true thing loudly: this is not a pyramid, it is a spike on a slab. If that is the message you want the page to deliver, this is the shape that delivers it.

Why D1 draws the ideal instead

In D1 the trapezoids are fixed geometry — they are the rule, not the data. The counts sit on each tier and the colour says which comparisons fail, so the shape stays legible at any set of numbers. The cost is that a reader could take the shape for the data and think the pyramid is healthier than it is, which is why every tier carries its own count and the two broken boundaries are labelled 39 ≥ 15 and 15 ≥ 13 rather than left to the fill colour alone.
